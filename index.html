Lilies, after rain

October 2026 
I keep the flowers my mother would have thrown away,
the ones bruised at the edges,
still opening anyway.

There is a kind of faith
in anything that blooms
after it has been told it is finished.

Emily is the part of me that only speaks in poems. This is our shared home: a night garden of poems, essays and articles that we keep tending.
