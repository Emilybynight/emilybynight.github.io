In a distant memory of a past life,
or a promised moment for this one, 
I see myself driving my car, the jeep 
In mountains of a faraway land

alone , or with company , I don't know 
I listen to the Miley Cyrus cover of "fade into you"
and I feel alive 
a little sad 
a little happy 
a little contemplative 

like my dad when he used to drive
us in the mountains 
When we were little
