---
title: How Much Strength Did It Take You to Be Soft?
date: 2026-10-28
language: English
cover: /assets/images/emily-poem-poster-a.jpg
---
You handled me very well…

I recall your calm attitude,
your loving words,
your smile.

I wonder — how did you get there?

As I’m going through my own mourning,
as I’m arriving late to our funeral,
I know how hard it is.
I feel sorry for what you must have been through
to show up like this…

How many difficult nights did you have to survive alone?
How many tears have you wept off your tired face?
How many times did you have to calm yourself
and comfort your pretty soul?
And how many times did you have to lie to your mind,
and beg it to be quiet?

I admire you,
Even in the darkest of my despair, even in these lonely days, 
I pray that we find the peace that we deserve…
