My mind can’t force anything on this heart,

Never had successfully done so,

Only made me as ever torn apart…

So im not giving him a say at this, He’s finally stepping off the whel,

To let my wise, wiser heart lead.

This mind of mine has been a terrible driver to be honest,

I only put him there because I thought he’s the earnest,

My heart was bleeding, couldn’t ask him for more,

Trembling in his corner, trying to make sense of it all…

Betrayed by all he knew, all he trusted before

High from confusion, heartbeats racing to fade…

Couldn’t really help but keep him far away

In the cold as his blood was thickening

That was my mistake.

Should have raced to his rescue,

Should have cleared his wounds, and told him

“Dear, I’ll be here for you “

Hug him long enough to know that’s its okay, its safe

And let him know it was never his mistake,

No, it was never a mistake to be this pure,

This kind, this loving, this caring in a world so obscure …

Should have told him not to listen to their voices,

And that nobody on earth is “wiser”,

Nobody knows it all,

and we’re all, sometimes, condemned to fall,

We all have dark places we’ve been to

And that we want to visit no more.

We’re all trying to make sense of it all,

Carrying unseen weights on our shoulders

Carefully balancing our steps,

Desperate to keep our heads tall.

So heart of mine, next time you doubt your inner light,

Remember that you already know wrong from right,

Please, Hold into that flame of your soul so tight,

And burn all the boxes you have to fit in, in sight

You are more than enough as you are on this earth,

And living happily, is not a right to be earned.
