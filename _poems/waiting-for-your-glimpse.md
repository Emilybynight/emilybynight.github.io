title: Waiting for your glimpse
date: 2026-10-29
language: English
---
 When you go ,you never look back…

I noticed this the first date, I greeted you goodbye…

waited for you to look back,

And did that each time...

It always fascinated me …

How you didn’t look back, at every and each time !

I wondered how ? And why?

Its a habit of one of a kind !

And deep down, I was afraid of thinking about it, 

deep down I knew you would be like this…

You wont look back if I'll let you go. Right?

And I never dared to ask you about it ,

Cause I knew your answer ,

You don't look back when you say goodbye…

And each time I was standing there ,

Looking back at you, at least three times ..

And waiting for your glimpse ,

But you never looked back ,

And I never asked you why … »
