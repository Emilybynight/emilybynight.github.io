Authenticity is selfish and jealous. 

She pulls you from your feet and makes you lose your balance. 

You fall into your head and hit the floor.

 It shakes up your world.

 It forces you to look at the skies above and realize how small you are, how indistinctive and irrelevant. 

It demands you and orders you to lose yourself, to let yourself go, to just be.

 It violently kicks out fear, hits you in the face of yours who desires praise and acceptance and validation from others. 

It holds you from your shoulders and turns you around to see yourself at the mirror as you are: naked and weak and ugly and incapable, emotionally unreliable, messy human self. 

And when you finally see that and understand how fundamentally flawed and sacred you are, and not only accept but celebrate your humanness, 

she then inhabits you and makes you her home.
